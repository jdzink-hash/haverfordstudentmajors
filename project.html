  2. How might we create clothing trends that are long-lasting rather than very short, to prevent waste?

Step 2

Wild Ideas / Bad Ideas

Before narrowing down, we pushed for the biggest, strangest and even worst answers to our question.

A squirrel that follows you around campusA costumed mascot who shouts deadlines at you between classes.
Apply to everything automaticallyOne button that sends your resume to every opportunity, whether it fits or not.
Deadline billboard on Lancaster AveA giant countdown sign everyone drives past.
A personal career agent for every studentLike a sports agent, but for summer internships.
Opportunity fortune cookies at the DCEvery meal comes with a random internship inside.
Email every listing to every studentThe opposite of our goal: maximum inbox overload.

What the bad ideas taught us

  • Students don't need more opportunities pushed at them. They need the right ones, filtered by major and class year.
  • Deadlines are the moment that matters, so they should be the first thing you see.
  • It has to live in one place, not spread across Handshake, email, department pages and Squirrel Space.
Step 3

Final Project Idea + Persona

FordFutures: one place to find every opportunity that fits your major.

What we made

A website that collects internships, research, fellowships, summer funding, graduate programs, career prep resources, events and clubs from across Haverford, and lets students filter them by major, type and class year. Every card shows the deadline, eligibility and a direct link to apply. Students can create a profile to get listings matched to them, track what they've signed up for and attended, and add events to Google Calendar.

Why

  • Opportunities are scattered across Handshake, CCPA emails, department websites, the course catalog and club pages.
  • It's hard to tell which ones fit your major or class year without opening each one.
  • Deadlines are easy to miss, especially for first-years who don't know what to look for yet.

Step 5

What We Tested & Learned

We kept changing the site as we used it and got feedback.

What we testedWhat we learned & changed
LogoA shield and a hand-drawn squirrel didn't feel right. A squirrel in a graduation cap was friendlier and more memorable, so it became the logo.
Button orderPeople look for the apply link first, so “Learn more & apply” now comes before tracking buttons, and event cards say “Learn more & register”.
Filter names“Summer Program” was confusing next to summer internships and funding, so it became “Summer Education Program”. We added Volunteer, Career Prep and Graduate Program types as new kinds of listings came in.
ClubsListing every club made the site cluttered. Keeping only career- and major-focused clubs kept it useful.
Remembering eventsMarking an event as signed up wasn't enough to remember it, so we added an “Add to Google Calendar” button.
Past deadlinesExpired listings cluttered results, so they're hidden by default but still viewable.

Next Steps & Support Needed

FordFutures works today. Here's what would make it last.

Next steps

  • Pull Haverford events from Handshake automatically instead of adding them by hand.
  • Send real email calendar invites when a student signs up.
  • Move profiles to a real database so they work on any device.
  • Run more testing with students from different majors and class years.

Support needed

  • CCPA partnership to post and update listings directly.
  • Handshake data access so events stay current.
